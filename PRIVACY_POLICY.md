# ShiftFlow Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

ShiftFlow is a work-shift scheduling application. This privacy policy explains how ShiftFlow handles information when you use the app.

## Data handled by the app

ShiftFlow can store schedule information that you enter or generate, including shift/day types, working hours, overtime, leave/rest classifications, notes, manually added holidays, cycle definitions, appearance preferences, and notification preferences.

This information is stored locally on your device. ShiftFlow does not require an account and does not send your schedule, notes, overtime, or other personal schedule data to a ShiftFlow server.

## Notifications

If you enable shift reminders, ShiftFlow uses Android notification functionality to schedule reminders locally on your device. Notification permission can be denied or disabled from Android settings.

## Backup and cycle files

ShiftFlow can create backup files and portable cycle-template files only when you choose to export them. You select where those files are saved or shared. Exported files are under your control and are not automatically uploaded by ShiftFlow.

## Data sharing and third parties

ShiftFlow does not sell user data and does not share personal schedule data with advertisers or data brokers. The Google Play version does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, account services, or a ShiftFlow-hosted cloud backend.

Google Play and the Android operating system may process information independently as part of app distribution, installation, security, and platform services. Their handling is governed by their own policies.

## Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on the device until it is changed or deleted by you, cleared through Android app settings, or removed when the app is uninstalled, subject to Android's own backup/restore behavior.

Files that you deliberately export, such as ShiftFlow backups, remain wherever you saved or shared them until you delete them yourself.

ShiftFlow does not maintain a server-side account or server-side personal schedule record, so there is no remote ShiftFlow account data to delete.

## Security

ShiftFlow uses Android application storage for its local data and Android system interfaces for file export/import and notifications. Keep exported backup files secure if they contain personal schedule information or notes.

## Children

ShiftFlow is a general productivity/scheduling tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If ShiftFlow's data handling changes, this policy will be updated to reflect the new behavior.
